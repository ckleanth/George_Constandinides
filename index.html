Fields of work

Seven connected disciplines, one method.

Describe the governing physics, then solve it — whether the domain is a combustion chamber, a landing gear, or the open ocean.

Computational Fluid Dynamics
Multiphase, reacting, Non Newtonian and electro-magneto-hydrodynamic flows, built and solved in ANSYS CFX.
Multi-Body Dynamics
Mechanism, suspension, and landing-gear dynamics modelled and correlated in MSC ADAMS.
Thermal Systems & Heat Transfer
Thermal management from the combustion chamber to the deep ocean.
Hydraulics & 1D Modelling
System-level models in AMESim and bespoke first-principles tools using Python or MATLAB for gas–liquid springs.
Systems Engineering
Requirements definition, validation, and verification of complex coupled systems.
Finite Element Analysis & CAD
Structural assessment and design geometry for loaded mechanical components.
Simulation Software
Engineering analysis and data-processing tools written in Python or MATLAB.

Selected work

Two decades of building models that predict.

A career plotted against time — from doctoral research to chartered practice across aerospace, industrial, and energy systems.

2024 — present

Research Associate

Cyprus University of Technology · Limassol

On the HydrOTEC project, developing Systems using 0-1D analytical and CFD models for green-hydrogen production driven by Solar-Assisted Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion — extracting useful work from the temperature difference between warm surface water and the cold deep ocean. Also a lecturer at the Technical University of Cyprus.

Analytical modelling Thermodynamics CFD 1D Thermal OTEC Hydrogen Teaching
2010 — 2024 · 14 years

Performance & Dynamics Engineer

Safran Landing Systems UK · ex Messier-Bugatti-Dowty

Lead modeller for aircraft landing-gear systems — extension/retraction, steering, and shock-absorber behaviour. Built and correlated multi-body dynamic models in MSC ADAMS coupled to hydraulic and control-system models, and maintained in-house prediction software for oleo-pneumatic (gas–liquid) springs. Supported requirements definition, verification and validation test campaigns, and the development of analysis methods and tools.

MSC ADAMS MBD Hydraulics Control systems Oleo-pneumatic springs V&V
2008 — 2010 · 2 yr 7 mo

CFD / Design Engineer

Tetronics · Swindon

Designed the internal cavity of industrial plasma converters and built the CFD models that simulate what happens inside them. The physics spanned electro-magneto-hydrodynamic plasma-arc dissociation and radiation, Eulerian–Lagrangian particle capture, combustion and reaction mechanisms, and non-Newtonian multiphase flow modelling the motion and solidification of molten metal and slag — all to optimise furnace geometry against real operating conditions.

ANSYS CFX CFD Plasma / EMHD Combustion Multiphase Non Newtonian Solidification
2005 — 2008

Research Engineer (PhD)

University of Birmingham · sponsored by Jaguar Cars

Doctoral research on the thermal management and control of an engine running in HCCI (Homogeneous Charge Compression Ignition) mode. Combined CFD with 1D thermal modelling and real-time control in MATLAB/Simulink, supported by engine experimentation.

HCCI MATLAB / Simulink 1D Thermal CFD Engine testing

Student CFD applications by Dr. George Constandinides

CFD in Applied Thermodynamics — By Hand, in CFX and in OpenFOAM

Two problems for students: a converging-diverging nozzle and an evaporative spray cooler. Students solve each by hand first, then in Ansys CFX and in OpenFOAM, judge every simulation against their own calculation, and see where the theory's assumptions stop holding.
